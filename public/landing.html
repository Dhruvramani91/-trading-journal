Questions, answered

What does the discipline score measure?

It shows how closely you followed the GXT or Lathyrus model on each trade. It rewards process, so a losing trade you handled well still counts.

Do I need to know the models already?

No. If you're still learning the GXT or Lathyrus style, PrecisionJournal helps you track your learning and your trades as you go.

How much does PrecisionJournal cost?

PrecisionJournal is free to use while it's in testing. Pricing will be announced soon.

Can I journal trades from different markets?

Yes. Log futures or forex with the same routine, and compare results by setup or day of the week.
